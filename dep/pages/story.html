02 / THE STORY

A fairly nonlinear
way of getting here.

My path through computer science has never been just about writing code.

It has moved between data, software, machine learning, hardware, design, communication and some wonderfully strange experiments along the way.

From building a shape-shifting mannequin prototype in 2019 to working with data analytics and cloud tooling, the common thread has been curiosity: understand the problem, learn what is necessary, then build something real.

THE THREAD

Learn → experiment → build → repeat.

That cycle has shaped most of my work. I am most comfortable when a project leaves room to investigate, prototype and improve rather than simply follow a predetermined recipe.
