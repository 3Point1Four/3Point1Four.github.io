01 / THE STORY

A fairly nonlinear
way of getting here.

My path through computer science has never been just about writing code. It has moved between data, software, machine learning, hardware, design, communication and some wonderfully strange experiments along the way.

I completed a BTech in Computer Science Engineering at MPSTME in Mumbai in 2024. Since then, I've worked across data analytics and software development, while continuing to build and explore systems on my own.

I enjoy taking an unfamiliar problem, breaking it down, learning whatever is necessary, and turning the result into something that actually works.
